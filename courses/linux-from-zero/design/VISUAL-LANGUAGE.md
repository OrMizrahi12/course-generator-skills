# Linux From Zero — visual language

Silent. 1920×1080. No narration.

## Surface
- Background: `#080C14` with a slow navy radial (`#152038`)
- Type: Inter
- Ink: `#F3EDE4`
- Mute: `#8B93A7`
- Accent (the part we are looking at): `#E8A53A`
- Rule / hairline: `rgba(243,237,228,0.12)`

## Wordmark
Top-left, always: `LINUX FROM ZERO` in 13px, letter-spacing 0.28em.
Lesson index top-right: `01` in accent.

## Pictures
Every physical thing is a **real photograph** (Wikimedia), not an icon.
Every brand (later lessons) uses its **real mark**.
Photo sits in a 28px-radius well `#10141E`, object-fit contain.

## Motion
- One thing arrives at a time
- 0.7–0.9s `power3.out`
- Hold long enough to read (about 8s per part)
- No bounce, no spin, no edge-crawling chrome

## Type sizes
- Giant part name: 112px / 0.92
- Sentence: 36px mute
- Title: 88px
